2 Portion Tray Sealer

A 2 compartment tray sealing machine for tiffin and lunch box trays, so dal and rice, or curry and bread, stay apart until the meal is eaten.

About the 2 Portion Tray Sealer

The 2 Portion Tray Sealer seals two-compartment trays with a single sheet of film. Both sections close at the same time, and the two foods do not mix in transit.

It is a good fit for tiffin services and small kitchens that send out a simple two-part meal every day.

Where it is used: Tiffin and lunch box services, dal and rice combos, curry and roti or bread meals, office and school meal orders, and small catering orders.

How to choose: Send us a sample tray or its length, width and compartment layout. We will confirm the right mould and machine.

Key features

  • Seals two-compartment trays in one press
  • Keeps the two foods separate and fresh
  • Mould made for your tray size
  • Stainless steel body, easy to clean
  • Simple controls, easy for any staff to use
