Round Container Sealer

A round container sealing machine for bowls and tubs, made for soups, biryani, curries, ice cream and sweets.

About the Round Container Sealer

The Round Container Sealer seals round bowls and tubs with film across the full rim. Gravy, soup and melted ice cream stay inside, even when the pack is carried on a bike or in a delivery bag.

One machine handles many food types. Change the mould to match your container and keep packing.

Where it is used: Soup and curry bowls, biryani and rice containers, raita and dips, ice cream tubs, sweets and mithai, dessert bowls and sauces.

How to choose: Send us a sample container or tell us its diameter and depth. We will match the right mould and confirm the machine.

Key features

  • Seals round bowls, tubs and containers with film
  • Full-rim seal that keeps liquids inside
  • Mould made to your container size
  • Stainless steel body, easy to clean
  • Simple controls and easy daily use
