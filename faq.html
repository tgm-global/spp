Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about food tray sealing machines, moulds, installation and prices.

What is a disposable food tray sealing machine?

It is a machine that places a plastic film over a disposable food tray, cup or container and seals it with heat and pressure. The sealed pack stays leak-proof and clean during delivery and takeaway.

Which machine seals tea cups, juice glasses and curd cups?

The Cup and Glass Sealer is made for plastic cups and glasses. It suits tea, lassi, juice, shakes, curd and dessert cups.

Can you seal 5 compartment and 8 compartment thali trays?

Yes. We have a 5 Portion Meal Tray Sealer and an 8 Portion Meal Tray Sealer for multi-compartment thali and meal trays. A 2 Portion Tray Sealer is also available for tiffin trays.

Can the machine be made for my tray size?

Yes. Each machine uses a mould made for your tray size. Send us a sample tray or a photo with the size, and we will match the right machine and mould.

Which machine is best for a cloud kitchen or catering business?

It depends on your container. Cloud kitchens and caterers usually choose the 2, 5 or 8 Portion Meal Tray Sealer for thali and meal boxes, or the Round Container Sealer for biryani and curry bowls.

Which sealing film should I use?

The right film depends on your container material. Tell us what trays or cups you use and we will guide you.

Do you help with installation and training?

Yes. Installation and setup help is included, and the controls are simple enough for your staff to learn quickly.

What power supply do the machines need?

Our machines run on a single-phase power supply. The power rating of each model is listed on its page.
