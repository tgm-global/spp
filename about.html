About Shri Parshwa Polymers

We supply tray sealing machines for disposable food containers from Sahibabad, Ghaziabad.

Who we are

Shri Parshwa Polymers is based at Ocean Plaza, Shalimar Garden, Sahibabad, Ghaziabad. We help cloud kitchens, caterers, tiffin services, sweet shops and juice bars seal their food safely with the right tray sealing machine.

Our range has six machines: a cup and glass sealer, a round container sealer, a rectangular tray sealer, and 2, 5 and 8 portion meal tray sealers. You can see them all on our products page.

A sealed pack protects food from spills and dust, and shows the customer that the food is untouched. Our goal is to make that quick, simple and affordable for every food business.

What we offer

  • Six tray sealing machines for cups, bowls, trays and thali meals
  • Moulds made for your container size
  • Advice on the right machine for your food and daily quantity
  • Installation and setup help
  • Support and spare parts that are easy to get

How we work

From your first call to your first sealed tray, in four simple steps.

  1. Tell us what you pack

    Share your tray or cup type, its size and how many you pack in a day.

  2. We suggest the machine

    We recommend the right machine and a mould that fits your container.

  3. Get the price

    We send the price on a call or on WhatsApp, with no confusion.

  4. Install and start

    We help with installation and setup so you can start sealing quickly.

Who we serve

Our machines are used by cloud kitchens, restaurants, caterers, tiffin services, hospital and factory canteens, sweet shops, bakeries, juice bars and tea stalls in Ghaziabad, Delhi NCR and beyond.

If you pack food for delivery or takeaway, a tray sealing machine will make your packing faster, cleaner and more professional.
