How to choose a food tray sealing machine

A tray sealing machine is chosen by the container you use. For tea, juice and lassi cups, the cup and glass sealing machine is the right fit. Bowls of soup, biryani and curry need a round container sealer, while salads, noodles and bakery trays use a rectangular tray sealer.

For tiffin and thali packing, pick a 2, 5 or 8 compartment meal tray sealing machine by the number of compartments on your tray. Every machine uses a mould made for your tray size.
