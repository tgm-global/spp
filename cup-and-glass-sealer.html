Cup and Glass Sealer

A cup and glass sealing machine that closes plastic cups with film in seconds, so tea, juice and lassi reach the customer without a spill.

About the Cup and Glass Sealer

The Cup and Glass Sealer closes disposable plastic cups and glasses with sealing film. A sealed cup does not spill inside a delivery bag, stays clean, and shows the customer that nobody has opened it.

It is a simple table-top machine. Place the filled cup under the head, press, and the film is sealed on the rim with even heat and pressure.

Where it is used: Tea and coffee stalls, juice and shake bars, lassi and buttermilk shops, curd and dessert makers, cold coffee and bubble tea shops, and any outlet that sells drinks for takeaway or home delivery.

How to choose: Tell us the diameter and height of your cup or glass and how many you pack in a day. We will confirm the right mould and the right machine for you.

Key features

  • Seals plastic cups and glasses with sealing film
  • Mould made for your cup size
  • Even heat for a firm, leak-proof seal
  • Stainless steel body, easy to wipe clean
  • Simple controls, no special training needed
